The Argument

Why this makes sense

Six steps from problem to PolyAI opportunity — the narrative you take into any internal or external conversation.

1

eBay sellers are drowning in buyer conversations

A high-volume eBay seller with 200+ listings receives multiple buyer inquiries every day — "What's your best price?", "Does this ship to Canada?", "Any scratches?". The same questions, over and over. 80% of eBay buyer inquiries are repetitive. A typical active seller spends 10–31 hours per month just on message handling — before accounting for Best Offer negotiation rounds. Some sellers are so fatigued by lowball Best Offers that they disable the feature entirely, missing serious buyers in the process.

↳ Every unanswered message is a lost sale
2

Response time is directly tied to revenue

eBay's own data shows sellers responding within 4 hours see 25% higher conversion rates. eBay now publicly displays seller response times on listings, creating algorithmic pressure to respond fast. Most sellers can't maintain sub-4-hour windows 24/7 — so they lose sales at 2am, on weekends, and during peak periods. A 1% improvement in seller rating translates to 15–25% sales uplift through improved search ranking and buyer confidence.

↳ 1% rating improvement = 15–25% more sales
3

Existing tools help sellers manage the inbox — they don't replace them

Tools like eDesk ($49–$69/mo) make the inbox faster to process. But a human still reads every message and clicks send. 3Dsellers' autoresponder is rule-based: keyword match → template reply, capped at 3 messages per conversation per day. No tool on the market can hold a real, contextual buyer conversation autonomously. No tool can negotiate a Best Offer back-and-forth in natural language. The gap is completely unaddressed.

↳ Incumbent tools are workflow managers, not agents
4

eBay has not invested in seller conversation AI

eBay has built impressive AI for listing creation (Magical Listing, used by 10M+ sellers) and launched a buyer-side shopping agent with OpenAI Operator (Jan 2026). But when it comes to seller-side buyer conversations, the gap is stark — eBay.ai only handles basic shipping and item detail questions, excludes negotiation entirely, and faces widespread seller resistance. No investment has gone into autonomous, conversational seller agents. That is the space PolyAI can own.

↳ eBay chose OpenAI for buyer-side — the seller conversation side is open for PolyAI
5

PolyAI's Open Platform is exactly the right product for this

PolyAI is rebranding around Open Platform — an omnichannel agent infrastructure that goes beyond voice to include messaging, chat, and digital channels, backed by a growing suite of out-of-the-box integrations. This is no longer a contact-centre-only play. The platform is built for any conversation surface — including the eBay Messaging API. Sellers configure their sales agent once — product knowledge, pricing floor, brand voice, return policy — and deploy it across every channel they operate. eBay is the enterprise customer. Sellers self-serve. PolyAI distributes via eBay's Developer Programme (340K+ registered developers) or App Marketplace. One enterprise deal unlocks 17M sellers across every channel they sell on.

↳ Open Platform + omnichannel messaging + integrations = the right product at the right time
6

Existing tools are helpers, not agents — the autonomous gap is real

Seller tools already exist and sellers pay for them: 3Dsellers, eDesk, ChannelReply. But they're rule-based inbox managers — a human still reads and sends every reply. PolyAI's CAIP competitors (Sierra, Cognigy, Parloa) are contact-centre-first and structurally can't scale to millions of individual sellers. Intercom/Fin is the closest risk — strong chat AI expanding into ecommerce — but has no eBay Messaging API and no understanding of auction or Best Offer dynamics. The benchmark proving autonomous seller conversation works at scale: Taobao's Dianxiaomi was used 1.5 billion times in a single shopping festival. The Western equivalent doesn't exist. PolyAI can build it — but the window will close.

↳ Seller tools exist — autonomous seller AI on eBay doesn't. That's the gap.

Demo Preparation

Three flows to show eBay

Each scenario maps directly to a documented seller pain point. All three are achievable with existing PolyAI capabilities + eBay Messaging API integration.

01

Best Offer Negotiator

Persona: Power seller, 500+ listings, receives 30+ Best Offers per week.

Flow: Buyer submits 60% of asking price. Agent responds conversationally — explains item condition and uniqueness, counters at 85%, handles the back-and-forth, closes the deal. All without seller involvement.

Why it lands: This is pure volume pain. Some sellers disable Best Offer entirely because of negotiation fatigue. This demo solves it.

02

24/7 Pre-Purchase Q&A

Persona: Refurbished electronics seller, 200 listings, lots of international buyers.

Flow: Buyer messages at 2am: "Any scratches? Will you ship to Canada? What's the return policy?" Agent answers all three instantly using listing data and seller-configured policies. Buyer buys. Seller was asleep.

Why it lands: Directly ties to the 25% conversion uplift from <4hr response. Every unanswered night-time message is a lost sale.

03

Post-Purchase Dispute Deflection

Persona: Clothing seller with high return volume, protecting Top Rated Seller status.

Flow: Buyer: "Item looks nothing like the photos." Agent acknowledges empathetically, asks for photos, checks return policy, offers resolution options (partial refund vs return), escalates to seller only when genuinely needed.

Why it lands: Negative feedback resolution costs 10–15 hours and damages algorithmic ranking. One avoided negative is worth the monthly subscription.

Open Questions

What to validate with eBay

Five questions to answer in the first conversation that determine deal structure and technical approach.

  • Does eBay's Messaging API support third-party agents responding autonomously on behalf of sellers, or is it read-only? This is the single most important technical question — determines build feasibility.
  • Who owns "seller tools" product at eBay? The OpenAI Operator partnership was led by the buyer/commerce team — is there a separate seller experience PM who would champion this?
  • Would eBay distribute PolyAI via their Developer Programme / App Marketplace, or is this a direct seller-acquisition motion? The answer changes pricing model, GTM, and support surface entirely.
  • With eBay now owning Depop (Feb 2026), could a single platform deal cascade across both marketplaces? Depop's 1.9M sellers + eBay's 17M = a materially larger contract.
  • What is eBay's internal AI roadmap for seller messaging beyond eBay.ai's current FAQ scope? We need to know if they plan to build this themselves before we go deep on the pitch.
